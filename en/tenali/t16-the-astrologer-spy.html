Episode T16

Will Crossing the River Kill the King? — The Astrologer Spy

🔮
Tenali Rama Stories Vijayanagara Empire Story of Wit
తెలుగుEnglishहिन्दी
📖 Reading time: ~5 min 👶 Age: 8+ 📂 Series: Tenali Stories ✍️ By: RayalSabha 📅 Published: 2026-10-25
Story Summary

Just as the army was about to march, an astrologer walked into the Vijayanagara court and warned King Krishnadevaraya: "If you cross the Tungabhadra river, your life is in danger!" The whole court was shaken. But with a single question, Tenali Ramakrishna turned the astrologer's own prediction against him. A gripping tale of how the truth exposed a deception dressed up as astrology.

01

The Astrologer's Warning — Danger Beyond the Tungabhadra!

The Royal Court

One day an astrologer came into the royal court of Vijayanagara. Looking at the King, he spoke gravely.

🔮 Astrologer
"Your Majesty… I see a danger in your horoscope."

Krishnadevaraya looked at him with interest.

👑 King
"What danger?"
🔮 Astrologer (slowly)
"Your Majesty, I hear you are going to war. If you cross the Tungabhadra river to the other side, your life will surely be in danger."
02

Alarm in the Court — The Minister's Fear

The Royal Court

That was all it took. The court was thrown into an uproar.

The King had already secretly decided to cross the Tungabhadra with his army and march toward Bijapur.

🏛️ Minister (cutting in at once)
"Your Majesty, this is dangerous! If the horoscope is true, what will become of the kingdom?"

The King fell into thought. Then he sent for Ramakrishna.

👑 King
"Ramakrishna… what do you make of his words?"
03

Ramakrishna's Suspicion — Not an Astrologer, but a Spy!

The Royal Court

Ramakrishna took one look at the astrologer — and smiled.

🪶 Tenali Rama
"My lord… this man is no astrologer."
👑 King (surprised)
"Then what is he?"
🪶 Tenali Rama
"A spy sent by Bijapur."

A sudden silence fell over the court.

👑 King
"How can you be so sure?"
🪶 Tenali Rama
"My lord… he arrived just as our army is about to set out. How does he know our war plans? Of all the things a horoscope could say, he spoke only of the one river we must cross. This is no coincidence."
04

How One Question Trapped the Astrologer

The Royal Court

Ramakrishna turned toward the astrologer.

🪶 Tenali Rama
"I will prove it."
🪶 Tenali Rama (calmly)
"If you are such a great astrologer… can you tell when your own death will come?"

The astrologer faltered for a moment, then answered quickly.

🔮 Astrologer
"I have thirty-four more years to live."
🪶 Tenali Rama (laughing)
"You know your own death — but the King's lifespan depends only on whether he crosses a river?"

Some in the court laughed. The astrologer glared angrily.

🪶 Tenali Rama
"If the King crosses the river, wins the war and returns safely, your punishment is certain."

The astrologer's face turned pale.

05

A Secret Letter with the Bijapur Seal

The Royal Court

Ramakrishna at once called for the guards.

🪶 Tenali Rama
"Search his bag and all his clothes."

In the search… a secret letter bearing the Bijapur seal was found, sewn and hidden in a knot of his garment.

Ramakrishna read the letter aloud: "The King must be stopped from crossing the Tungabhadra."

06

The Truth Comes Out — The King's Judgement

The Royal Court

The King's expression changed. Now everyone understood that Ramakrishna had spoken the truth.

👑 King (looking at the astrologer)
"It was never about danger to my life… your real aim was to stop my army from marching forward!"

The guards led the astrologer away as a prisoner. Ramakrishna bowed.

🪶 Tenali Rama
"My lord… we must not believe every word just because an astrologer said it. Only when we seek out the truth do we understand what is really going on."

The King looked at Ramakrishna with admiration.

Moral of the Story 🔮

Never blindly believe words meant to frighten you — only when you seek out the truth do you understand what is really going on.

💡 Life Lesson

When someone tries to scare us away from a decision, ask once: 'Who gains from this?' A single calm question can expose even the biggest deception.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the moral of this story?
+

Don't blindly believe frightening words — seek the truth. Not every word is true just because an astrologer says it.

How did Ramakrishna know the astrologer was a spy?
+

He arrived just as the army was leaving and spoke only of the Tungabhadra — part of a secret war plan. That was no coincidence, and the Bijapur-sealed letter found on him proved it.

What question did Ramakrishna ask?
+

He asked, 'Can you tell when you will die?' When the astrologer said 34 more years, Ramakrishna warned that he would be punished if the King returned safely — trapping him in his own words.

Is this story suitable for children?
+

Yes — it teaches children aged 8 and up to think, question, and not give in to blind belief.
