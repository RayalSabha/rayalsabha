Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit rayalsabha.com ("RayalSabha", "we") and how it is used.

Information we collect

You can read every story without an account, and we do not ask for your name, email or any personal details. If you email us, we use your email only to reply to you.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit and which stories they read. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as pages visited, approximate location, device and browser type. This information is aggregated and does not identify you personally. Learn more in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads, which helps keep the stories free.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at aboutads.info.

Children's privacy

Our stories are made for families and children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we do not ask anyone to register or submit personal details. Parents who believe a child has sent us personal information can email us and we will delete it.

YouTube videos

Story pages embed videos from our YouTube channel. When you play a video, YouTube (owned by Google) may set cookies under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The stories will still work without them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
