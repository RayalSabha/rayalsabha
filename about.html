About RayalSabha

Stories of wit and wisdom from India — in Telugu, English and Hindi

RayalSabha is a small storytelling studio. We retell India's best-loved moral stories — the wit of Tenali Ramakrishna in King Krishnadevaraya's court, Chanakya's timeless strategy, Akbar and Birbal's riddles, short Pitta Kathalu fables and Jeevita Kathalu life lessons — with scene-by-scene illustrations, a clear moral and a full narrated video for each story.

What you'll find here

  • Tenali Ramakrishna stories in Telugu — also in English and Hindi
  • Chanakya Niti, Akbar–Birbal, Pitta Kathalu and Jeevita Kathalu
  • Narrated videos on our YouTube channel, @Rayalsabha

Who the stories are for

Children (most stories suit ages 6 and up), parents, grandparents, teachers, and Telugu families living abroad who want to share our stories with the next generation. Every story is free to read.

How we make them

Each story is written and checked in Telugu first, then illustrated and narrated. Illustrations are created with digital and AI-assisted tools and edited by our team. English and Hindi editions follow the Telugu original.
